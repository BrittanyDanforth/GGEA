┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  CASH PRODUCT PURCHASE (e.g., 1,000 Cash)                      │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  Player clicks "BUY" in shop
         │
         ├─→ Client: MarketplaceService:PromptProductPurchase()
         │
         ├─→ Roblox: Shows purchase prompt
         │
         ├─→ Player: Completes purchase
         │
         ├─→ Server: MoneyShop.server.lua
         │          └─→ ProcessReceipt callback fires
         │              └─→ Grants cash via _G.AddPlayerMoney
         │
         └─→ Client: PromptProductPurchaseFinished
                    └─→ Plays success sound
                    └─→ Button resets to "BUY"
                    └─→ ✅ DONE!


┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  GAMEPASS PURCHASE (e.g., Auto Collect)                        │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  Player clicks "BUY" in shop
         │
         ├─→ Client: MarketplaceService:PromptGamePassPurchase()
         │
         ├─→ Roblox: Shows purchase prompt
         │
         ├─→ Player: Completes purchase
         │
         ├─→ Server: SanrioShopServer.lua
         │          └─→ PromptGamePassPurchaseFinished fires
         │              └─→ Waits 0.5s
         │              └─→ Verifies ownership
         │              └─→ Fires GamepassPurchased event
         │
         └─→ Client: Receives GamepassPurchased event
                    └─→ Clears ownership cache
                    └─→ Waits 0.3s
                    └─→ Destroys old gamepass items
                    └─→ Recreates all gamepass items
                    └─→ Auto Collect now shows:
                        ├─→ Row 1: "OWNED" (green)
                        └─→ Row 2: "ON/OFF" toggle ✅
                    └─→ Plays success sound
                    └─→ ✅ DONE!
